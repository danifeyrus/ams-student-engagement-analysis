# Figures and tables

The files in this directory are generated by `src/08_publication_assets.R`. They provide a compact selection of the full results for the written report and presentation. Source figures remain in `figures/`, while the underlying numerical outputs remain in `results/`.

## Figures

| File | Suggested title | Content | Suitable for | Caption note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `figures/02_cohort_inclusion_flow.png` | Analytical cohort | Enrollment counts at each eligibility step | Report, presentation | The primary 28-day cohort contains 2,011 of 2,498 recorded enrollments; 260 early unregistrations are excluded |
| `figures/04_feature_distributions_original.png` | Early engagement distributions | Original-scale clicks, active days, and resources | Report | The counts are skewed and include genuine zeros, which motivates the `log1p` transformation |
| `figures/06_feature_correlation.png` | Dependence among engagement measures | Pairwise relationships between transformed variables | Report | The three variables are strongly related and mainly describe a common intensity direction |
| `figures/08_mclust_bic_grid.png` | Gaussian-mixture model selection | BIC across component counts and covariance structures | Report, presentation | BIC selects G=4 VVE; this is evidence for a density model, not proof of four natural populations |
| `figures/09_mclust_profiles.png` | Early engagement profiles | Location and overlap of the four fitted components | Report, presentation | Median activity increases from P1 to P4, although the components overlap substantially |
| `figures/10_mclust_uncertainty.png` | Posterior classification uncertainty | Distribution of assignment uncertainty | Report, presentation | Mean uncertainty is 0.217 and 988 assignments exceed 0.20 |
| `figures/12_outcomes_by_profile.png` | Recorded outcomes by profile | Outcome proportions after clustering | Report, presentation | Outcome differences are descriptive associations and should not be interpreted causally |
| `figures/14_tclust_configuration_sensitivity.png` | TCLUST sensitivity | Agreement and cluster sizes across trimming and restriction settings | Report | Retained-case ARI stays between 0.117 and 0.283, and some solutions are highly unbalanced |
| `figures/17_method_disagreement_crosstab.png` | Comparison of Gaussian mixture and TCLUST | Retained-case cross-tabulation of the two partitions | Report, presentation | TCLUST forms one broad middle group instead of reproducing the Gaussian boundaries |
| `figures/19_mclust_subsample_stability.png` | Subsample stability | Fixed-model and model-reselected results across 25 samples | Report, presentation | The imposed specification is more stable than the complete model-selection procedure |

For a short presentation, Figures 08, 09, 10, 12, and either 17 or 19 cover the main argument. A written report can add Figures 02, 04, 06, and 14 together with Tables 01, 03, 05, 07, 09, and 11. Profile and outcome figures should be presented alongside at least one uncertainty or stability result.
